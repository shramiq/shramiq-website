About SHRAMIQ

Connecting businesses with the right people and helping job seekers discover better industrial opportunities.

Who We Are

SHRAMIQ is an industrial recruitment and staffing platform focused on connecting factories, companies and businesses with suitable candidates.

Our goal is simple — make recruitment easier for employers and help job seekers find suitable employment opportunities based on their skills, experience and preferred location.

Our Mission

We aim to build a reliable bridge between industrial employers and the workforce they need.

By collecting candidate profiles and understanding employer requirements, SHRAMIQ works towards making the hiring process faster, organised and more efficient.

What We Value

Right People

Helping employers connect with candidates whose skills match their workforce requirements.

Right Jobs

Helping candidates discover opportunities that match their skills, experience and preferences.

Better Future

Building long-term connections between businesses, workers and growing industries.
